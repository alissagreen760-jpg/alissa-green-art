About the work

My paintings explore the tension between the natural world and the man-made. I approach each piece like a diagram, looking for the shared symbols and feel across language, abstraction, geometry, music and nature, and bring together fine art, biological forms and vibrational energy to build abstract worlds that hold both the chaos and the calm of everyday life.
